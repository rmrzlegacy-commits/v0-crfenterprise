CRF Enterprise — Government & Enterprise Technology Contractor

CRF Enterprise is a SAM-registered, MWBE-certified government and enterprise technology contractor headquartered in Las Vegas, NV. CAGE Code: 107F5. We deliver secure software development, cloud infrastructure, digital transformation, and strategic consulting for federal agencies, state and local governments, and enterprise clients across the United States.

Core Capabilities

  • Custom Software Development (NAICS 541511) — mission-critical applications for government and enterprise
  • Computer Systems Design Services (NAICS 541512) — cloud-native architecture, FedRAMP-aligned infrastructure
  • Other Computer Related Services (NAICS 541519) — legacy modernization, DevSecOps, ATO support
  • Administrative Management Consulting (NAICS 541611) — IT strategy, enterprise architecture, acquisition advisory
  • Commercial & Institutional Building Construction (NAICS 236220) — government facility development
  • Highway, Street & Bridge Construction (NAICS 237310) — civil infrastructure for government projects

Government Credentials

  • SAM.gov Registered — Active FY2026. Eligible for federal contract awards.
  • CAGE Code: 107F5 — DoD and federal procurement identifier issued by the Defense Logistics Agency.
  • MWBE Certified — Minority Women Business Enterprise, eligible for set-aside programs.
  • FISMA / NIST RMF Aligned — Deliverables comply with NIST SP 800-53 and agency security baselines.
  • FAR / DFARS Compliant — Representations and Certifications available on request.
  • DevSecOps Delivery — Aligned with OMB M-21-31 and CISA guidance.

Services

Enterprise Technology Solutions

Custom software development, legacy system modernization, Authority to Operate (ATO) support, and enterprise systems integration. We build secure, scalable platforms under FISMA, NIST RMF, and FedRAMP frameworks for federal and enterprise clients.

Infrastructure Development

Cloud infrastructure design on AWS, Azure, and GCP. FedRAMP-ready environments, zero-trust network architecture, disaster recovery, and physical civil infrastructure for government facilities and transportation projects.

Digital Transformation

Legacy system modernization, process automation, citizen-facing portal design, and 21st Century IDEA Act compliance. We guide agencies through migration from monolithic to microservices architectures with minimal operational disruption.

Strategic Consulting

Technology strategy, IT capital planning, CPIC, enterprise architecture (TOGAF, DoDAF, FEA), compliance advisory, and federal acquisition support. Helping agencies align technology investments with mission objectives.
